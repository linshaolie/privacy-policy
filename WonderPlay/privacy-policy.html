中文

1. 适用范围

本隐私政策适用于 iOS 应用“奇趣玩 / WonderPlay”。奇趣玩是一组为儿童和亲子陪伴设计的互动小游戏，包括点点点、颜色魔法和弹射球门等玩法。

2. 我们是否收集个人信息

当前版本不会收集个人信息。应用不需要注册或登录，不提供云同步，不集成广告 SDK，不集成第三方分析 SDK，也不包含应用内购买。

3. 本地偏好

应用可能会在设备本地保存你选择的语言偏好，以便下次打开时继续使用。该偏好只保存在设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器。

4. 麦克风权限

点点点游戏可能会请求麦克风权限，用于识别吹气和拍手等声音强度变化，并据此触发游戏反馈。音频只在设备本地用于实时判断声音强度。

  • 应用不会录制或保存麦克风音频。
  • 应用不会上传或分享麦克风音频。
  • 应用不会在未授权时使用麦克风。

5. 数据是否离开设备

当前版本没有账号、云同步、广告、分析或网络上传功能。语言偏好和游戏过程中的临时状态留在设备本地。我们不会出售个人数据，也不会将你的数据分享给第三方。

如果你启用了 iOS 设备备份，应用的本地数据可能会作为设备备份的一部分由 Apple 处理。该过程受你的设备设置和 Apple 的相关政策约束。

6. 儿童与家庭使用

奇趣玩面向儿童和亲子陪伴场景设计。当前版本不会向我们收集儿童个人信息。建议儿童在家长或监护人陪伴下使用本应用。

7. 数据保留与删除

本地语言偏好会保留在设备上，直到你更改设置、清除应用数据或卸载应用。卸载应用通常会删除应用本地数据。

8. 政策更新

如果未来版本加入账号、云同步、分析、崩溃收集、网络服务或其他会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会更新本隐私政策，并在需要时更新 App Store 的隐私信息。

9. 联系我们

如果你对本隐私政策有问题，请通过以下方式联系：l_charlie@163.com

English

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS app “WonderPlay / 奇趣玩”. WonderPlay is a collection of interactive mini games designed for children and family play, including Dot Dot Dot, Color Magic, and Sling Goal.

2. Personal Information

The current version does not collect personal information. The app does not require registration or login, does not provide cloud sync, does not include advertising SDKs, does not include third-party analytics SDKs, and does not include in-app purchases.

3. Local Preferences

The app may store your selected language preference locally on your device so it can remember the setting next time. This preference stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Microphone Permission

Dot Dot Dot may ask for microphone permission to detect sound intensity changes, such as blowing or clapping, and use them as gameplay input. Audio is processed on device only for real-time sound intensity detection.

  • The app does not record or save microphone audio.
  • The app does not upload or share microphone audio.
  • The app does not use the microphone without permission.

5. Does Data Leave the Device?

The current version has no account system, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or upload feature. Language preferences and temporary gameplay state remain local to the device. We do not sell personal data or share your data with third parties.

If you enable iOS device backup, local app data may be included in backups handled by Apple. That process is controlled by your device settings and Apple's applicable policies.

6. Children and Family Use

WonderPlay is designed for children and family play. The current version does not collect children's personal information from users. We recommend that children use the app with a parent or guardian.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local language preferences remain on your device until you change settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app usually removes local app data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, network services, or any other feature that changes how data is processed, we will update this Privacy Policy and, when required, update the App Store privacy information.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: l_charlie@163.com
