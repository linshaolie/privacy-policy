Yue Crosstalk

whenever you need it.

A crosstalk player with a built-in catalogue, favorites, listening history, playback controls, and a sleep timer. Browse the included catalogue, save favorites, and pick up where you left off. Playback controls, background audio, and a sleep timer help you listen in the way that suits you.

Built-in catalogue

Browse or search the included crosstalk collection and play a selection.

Favorites and history

Save favorites and return to recent listening. These records are stored on your device.

Use playback modes, adjust speed, listen in the background, and set a sleep timer.
