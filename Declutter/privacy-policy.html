生效日期：2026 年 6 月 6 日
Effective date: June 6, 2026

中文 English
Declutter 以本地整理台账为核心。当前版本不需要账号登录，主要数据默认保存在设备本地。

中文

1. 适用范围

本隐私政策适用于 iOS 应用“断舍离 / Declutter”。Declutter 用于记录日常物品、整理分类、查看闲置阈值，并帮助你回顾已经处理、转赠、出售或捐献的物品记录。

2. 我们处理哪些数据

Declutter 当前版本主要处理以下数据：

  • 物品台账：物品名称、分类、位置、备注、上次使用日期、阈值、状态、处理方式和处理历史。
  • 物品照片：你主动拍摄或从相册选择后绑定到物品的图片。
  • 应用设置：例如默认阈值、提醒窗口、分类、位置和广告/会员解锁状态。

3. 数据保存位置

上述核心内容默认保存在你的设备本地，当前版本不要求注册账号，也不提供自建云同步。你在应用中记录的物品、分类、位置和照片，主要用于你自己查看、筛选、整理和回顾。

4. 权限使用

  • 相机权限：仅在你主动选择“拍照”录入物品图片时使用。
  • 相册权限：仅在你主动从相册选择图片时使用。
  • 追踪权限：如果应用版本启用了广告相关能力，系统可能会在适用场景下请求追踪授权，用于支持广告展示或衡量。你可以拒绝该权限。

5. 广告与内购

Declutter 当前代码版本包含激励广告和一次性去广告/永久解锁类内购能力。若你使用广告或购买相关功能，第三方服务提供商可能会处理有限的设备、请求、交易或反欺诈信息，以完成广告加载、展示、购买、支付确认和权益恢复。

这些处理通常由 Apple 的 StoreKit 或广告服务提供商根据其各自政策完成。我们不会因为你记录的物品名称、备注或照片内容而主动把整套台账上传到自有服务器。

6. 数据共享

除完成广告展示、内购处理、设备备份或法律要求所需的基础技术流程外，我们不会主动出售你的个人数据，也不会把你本地记录的物品台账整体提供给第三方。

7. 数据保留与删除

物品记录和照片会保留在你的设备上，直到你在应用内删除对应内容或卸载应用。卸载应用通常会删除应用本地数据。若你开启了 iOS 设备备份，本地数据也可能作为备份的一部分由 Apple 按你的设备设置进行处理。

8. 儿童与家庭使用

Declutter 可被个人或家庭用于整理家中物品，但当前版本并不面向儿童单独建立账号体系，也不会主动向我们服务器收集儿童个人信息。

9. 安全

我们尽量减少不必要的数据离开设备的情况，并优先采用本地存储。请你同时为设备启用密码、Face ID 或 Touch ID，以帮助保护本地台账和照片内容。

10. 政策更新

如果未来版本加入账号、云同步、分析、崩溃收集、客服工单、更多广告能力或其他会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会相应更新本隐私政策，并在需要时同步更新 App Store 隐私信息。

English

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS app “Declutter / 断舍离”. Declutter helps users log household items, organize categories and locations, review inactivity thresholds, and track how items were discarded, given away, donated, sold, or kept.

2. Data Processed by the App

The current version of Declutter mainly processes:

  • Item records: item names, categories, locations, notes, last-used dates, thresholds, statuses, disposal intents, and disposal history.
  • Item photos: images that you actively capture or choose from your photo library for a specific item.
  • App settings: such as default thresholds, warning windows, categories, locations, and ad or membership unlock status.

3. Where Data Is Stored

This core content is stored locally on your device by default. The current version does not require account registration and does not provide a self-hosted cloud sync service. Your logged items, categories, locations, and photos are mainly used for your own viewing, filtering, organizing, and review.

4. Permissions

  • Camera permission: used only when you actively choose to take a photo for an item.
  • Photo library permission: used only when you actively choose an image from your library.
  • Tracking permission: if an app version enables ad-related capabilities, the system may request tracking authorization where applicable to support ad delivery or measurement. You may decline that permission.

5. Ads and In-App Purchases

The current code version of Declutter includes rewarded ads and a one-time purchase flow for permanent unlock or ad removal. If you use ad or purchase features, third-party service providers may process limited device, request, transaction, or anti-fraud information to complete ad loading, ad presentation, purchases, payment confirmation, and entitlement restoration.

Those flows are typically handled by Apple StoreKit or advertising service providers under their own policies. We do not proactively upload your full item ledger, item names, notes, or photo content to our own servers simply because you use the app to organize items.

6. Data Sharing

Except for basic technical flows needed to provide ads, process in-app purchases, support device backups, or comply with legal requirements, we do not proactively sell your personal data or provide your full local item records to third parties.

7. Retention and Deletion

Item records and photos remain on your device until you delete the related content in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app usually removes its local data. If you enable iOS device backup, local app data may also be handled by Apple as part of that backup according to your device settings.

8. Children and Family Use

Declutter may be used by individuals or families to organize belongings, but the current version does not create a child-specific account system and does not intentionally collect children's personal information on our own servers.

9. Security

We try to minimize cases where data leaves your device and prioritize on-device storage. Please also protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID to help secure your local ledger and photos.

10. Changes to This Policy

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, customer support workflows, more advertising capabilities, or any other feature that changes how data is processed, we will update this Privacy Policy and, when required, update the App Store privacy information accordingly.
