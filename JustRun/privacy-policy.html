3. 本地数据与音频

  • 本地音乐库：应用内音乐和曲目信息随应用提供；本应用据此建立曲目列表，不要求你上传自己的音乐文件。
  • 播放记录：收藏、最近播放、播放次数和相关曲目信息保存在本地数据库，用于显示音乐列表和播放历史。
  • 偏好设置：播放模式、速度、定时关闭和去广告权益状态等信息保存在设备上。
  • 后台播放：当你主动播放音乐时，音频可以在切换应用或锁屏后继续播放；系统媒体控制可显示当前曲目信息并控制播放。

4. 第三方服务

  • Google Analytics for Firebase：用于汇总应用启动、功能交互和广告相关事件。Google 可能自动处理应用实例标识符、设备和应用技术信息。
  • Google Mobile Ads：用于提供应用内广告。该 SDK 及其合作方可能为广告投放、效果衡量和反欺诈处理设备、广告、网络及诊断数据。应用也会向 Google 服务端点发送轻量连通性检查请求，帮助诊断广告网络是否可达；这些请求会携带正常网络请求所需的信息，例如 IP 地址。
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：处理商品信息、付款、恢复购买和交易状态。本应用会把应用收据及 IDFV 发送给 Apple 的验证服务，以核验购买权益；我们不会取得你的完整银行卡信息。

第三方服务依据各自政策处理数据：Google 隐私权政策、Firebase 隐私与安全说明、Apple 隐私政策。

5. 广告与隐私选择

广告类型和个性化程度可能因适用法律、地区、设备设置、Google 设置和你的选择而不同。你可以通过 iOS“隐私与安全性”设置管理跟踪权限及广告相关选项；若本应用在你所在地区提供广告同意或隐私选项界面，也可以在那里调整选择。限制个性化广告不一定会移除广告。你也可以通过 Apple 的一次性 App 内购买移除应用内广告。

6. 保存期限、删除与你的权利

  • 本地曲目数据和设置通常保留到你在应用中清除内容、重置设备数据或卸载本应用。卸载通常会删除本地数据库；系统备份是否包含应用数据取决于你的设备与 iCloud 设置。
  • 分析、广告和技术日志由相应服务提供方依照运营、安全、合同和法律要求保存。
  • Apple 按其政策保存购买和交易记录；本应用仅在设备上保存提供去广告权益所需的状态。
  • 你可以联系开发者提出适用法律赋予的访问、更正、删除、限制处理或反对处理请求。对于由 Apple 或 Google 独立控制的数据，你可能需要直接使用其隐私工具或联系相应服务商。

7. 数据共享、出售与披露

我们不会出售你的本地收藏或播放历史。为提供分析、广告、购买处理与验证、客户支持、安全及法律合规，我们可能让上述服务提供方处理必要信息。法律要求、保护用户或开发者权利，或发生合并、收购及资产转移时，也可能依法披露或转移必要信息。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用并非专门面向儿童设计，也不会有意要求儿童提供真实身份信息。若监护人认为儿童通过邮件向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们以便核实并采取适当措施。

9. 安全与跨境处理

我们采取与应用规模及数据性质相适应的措施保护信息，但没有任何存储或传输方式能够保证绝对安全。Google、Apple 或邮件服务提供方可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理数据，并依据其政策、合同和适用法律提供保护。

English

1. Scope and Provider

This policy applies to the iOS app “Just Run” (the “App”), provided by independent developer Shaolie Lin. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact l_charlie@163.com.

2. Data We Process

  • On-device data: favorites, recently played tracks, play counts, playback mode and speed, sleep-timer settings, and app preferences are primarily stored locally to provide music playback and history.
  • Analytics data: app launches, screen and feature interactions, playback-setting actions, ad requests and outcomes, purchase outcomes, and technical information such as app version, device type, and operating-system version may be processed by Google Analytics for Firebase. Google may also automatically process an app-instance identifier and technical information.
  • Advertising and connectivity data: Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process ad requests, impressions, clicks, device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, network information, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The App also sends lightweight connectivity checks to Google service endpoints to diagnose ad-network reachability; these requests include information ordinarily needed for a network request, such as an IP address. Processing depends on region, device, and Google settings.
  • Purchase data: Apple processes product identifiers, transaction status, and receipts for the one-time remove-ads purchase and purchase restoration. The App’s receipt-validation request also sends your device’s identifierForVendor (IDFV) to Apple.
  • Support data: if you email us, we process the email address, message, device and operating-system details, and attachments you choose to provide so we can respond.

No account is required. The App does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data, and does not record running routes, distance, or fitness metrics. Do not send passwords, complete payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information by email.

3. Local Data and Audio

The App includes its music catalog and track information. Favorites, recently played items, play counts, playback preferences, and related track metadata are stored in a local database. User-initiated audio may continue in the background, and system media controls may display the current track and control playback. The App does not ask you to upload your own music files.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand app launches, feature interactions, and advertising-related events.
  • Google Mobile Ads provides in-app advertising and may process device, advertising, network, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The App also sends lightweight connectivity checks to Google service endpoints to diagnose ad-network reachability; those requests include ordinary network request information such as an IP address.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit processes product information, payment, purchase restoration, and transaction status. The App sends the app receipt and IDFV to Apple’s verification service to validate the remove-ads entitlement. We do not receive your full card details.

These providers process information under their own policies: Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising Choices

Ad type and personalization may depend on applicable law, region, device and Google settings, and your choices. You can manage tracking permissions and available advertising choices in iOS Privacy & Security settings. Where the App presents an advertising consent or privacy-options interface in your region, you may also manage choices there. Limiting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads. You may purchase Apple’s one-time remove-ads option.

6. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

  • Local track data and preferences generally remain until you clear them in the App, reset device data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling usually deletes the local database; device backups may include App data depending on your Apple and iCloud settings.
  • Analytics, advertising, and technical logs are retained by the relevant providers according to operational, security, contractual, and legal requirements.
  • Apple retains purchase and transaction records under its policies. The App stores only the on-device status needed to provide the remove-ads entitlement.
  • You may email us to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection rights. For data independently controlled by Apple or Google, you may need to use their privacy tools or contact them directly.

7. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your local favorites or playback history. Necessary information may be processed by the providers above for analytics, advertising, purchase handling and validation, support, security, and legal compliance. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

8. Children

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly request real-world identity information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information to us by email may contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Security and International Processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the App’s scale and the nature of the data, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google, Apple, or email providers may process data outside your country under their policies, contracts, and applicable law.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change and will post the new effective date here. Privacy questions and rights requests: l_charlie@163.com.
