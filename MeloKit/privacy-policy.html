隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日 MeloKit 由独立开发者 Shaolie Lin 提供。

我们收集什么

MeloKit 无需注册或登录。和弦查询、和弦试听及节拍器在设备上运行。我们不通过本应用收集、上传、出售或分享个人数据，不接入广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

设备上的数据

应用使用本地存储保存语言、和弦选择、节拍器参数及练习偏好。这些数据不会由 MeloKit 发送给开发者。系统备份可能包含这些设置，备份由 Apple 及您的设备设置管理。删除应用可清除设备上的应用数据；从备份恢复时，设置也可能恢复。

权限与音频

应用只播放内置音频，不录音，不请求麦克风、相机、通讯录或位置权限。后台音频用于在切换应用或锁屏时继续播放您启动的节拍器，可在应用中暂停。

联系我们与外部页面

如果您主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址和您提供的内容，仅用于处理问题，并仅在处理请求所需期间保留。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。打开支持或隐私网页时，浏览器及网站托管服务商可能按其各自政策处理 IP 地址等访问日志；这与应用内的离线功能不同。

儿童与政策更新

本应用不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策及生效日期。

隐私问题、支持请求或邮件资料删除请求，请联系：l_charlie@163.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 MeloKit is provided by independent developer Shaolie Lin.

Data collection

MeloKit requires no account or sign-in. Chord lookup, chord playback and the metronome run on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share personal data through the app. The app includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data on your device

Local storage remembers your language, chord selections, metronome parameters and practice preferences. MeloKit does not send these settings to the developer. System backups may include these settings and are managed by Apple and your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a backup may restore preferences.

Permissions and audio

The app plays bundled audio, does not record audio and does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access. Background audio lets a metronome you started continue when you switch apps or lock your device. You can pause it in the app.

Support and external pages

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the content you provide, use it only to handle your request and retain it only as needed for that purpose. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. When you open a support or privacy webpage, your browser and the website host may process access logs such as IP addresses under their own policies. This is separate from the offline app features.

Children and updates

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We will update this policy and its effective date if features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, support or deletion of correspondence, contact l_charlie@163.com.
