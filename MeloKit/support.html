常见问题

没有声音？请检查媒体音量、蓝牙或耳机输出，并尝试重新启动播放。 节拍器如何停止？打开节拍器页点击暂停，或使用和弦页的迷你节拍器暂停按钮。 如何切换语言？前往「设置 → 应用语言」，选择简体中文或 English。 需要联网或注册吗？和弦和节拍器均可离线使用，无需注册。 如何清除设置？删除应用可清除本机数据；恢复系统备份可能恢复原设置。

Frequently asked questions

No sound? Check media volume and your Bluetooth or headphone output, then restart playback. To stop the metronome, tap Pause on the Metronome tab or in the mini metronome on the Chords tab. Change language in Settings → App Language. Chord lookup and the metronome work offline without an account. Deleting the app clears local preferences; restoring a system backup may restore them.

反馈时请提供设备型号、iOS 版本、MeloKit 版本和复现步骤。请避免发送敏感信息。
When reporting an issue, include your device model, iOS and app versions, and steps to reproduce. Avoid sensitive information.
