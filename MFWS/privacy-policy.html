3. 本地数据与播放

  • 内置曲库：曲目文件和曲目信息随应用提供，播放时从设备本地读取。
  • 播放记录：收藏、最近播放、播放次数和曲目信息保存在本地数据库，用于显示曲目列表与播放历史。
  • 偏好设置：播放模式、速度、定时关闭和去广告权益状态等信息保存在设备上。
  • 后台播放：当你主动播放时，音频可以在切换应用或锁屏后继续播放；系统媒体控制可显示当前曲目信息并控制播放。

4. 第三方服务

  • Google Analytics for Firebase：用于汇总应用启动、功能交互、播放、购买和广告相关事件。Google 可能自动处理应用实例标识符、设备标识符（可能包括 IDFV）及设备和应用技术信息。
  • Google Mobile Ads：提供应用内广告。该 SDK 及其合作方可能为广告投放、效果衡量和反欺诈处理设备、广告、网络及诊断数据。应用还会向 Google 服务端点发送轻量连通性检查请求，以诊断广告网络是否可达；请求会包含 IP 地址等正常网络通信信息。
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：处理商品信息、付款、恢复购买和交易状态。本应用会将应用收据和 IDFV 发送给 Apple 的验证服务，以核验去广告权益；我们不会取得你的完整银行卡信息。

第三方服务依据各自政策处理数据：Google 隐私权政策、Firebase 隐私与安全说明和 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 广告与隐私选择

广告类型和个性化程度可能因适用法律、地区、设备设置、Google 设置和你的选择而不同。本应用目前没有单独的应用内广告隐私设置页；你可以通过 iOS“隐私与安全性”中的“跟踪”设置管理跨 App 跟踪权限。限制跟踪或个性化广告不一定会移除广告，也不一定停止为广告交付、安全和衡量所需的所有处理。你可以通过 Apple 的一次性 App 内购买移除应用内广告。

6. 保存期限、删除与你的权利

  • 本地曲目数据和设置通常保留到你重置设备数据或卸载本应用。卸载通常会删除本地数据库；系统备份是否包含应用数据取决于你的 Apple 设备与 iCloud 设置。
  • 分析、广告和技术日志由相应服务提供方依照运营、安全、合同和法律要求保存。
  • Apple 按其政策保存购买和交易记录；本应用仅在设备上保存提供去广告权益所需的状态。
  • 你可以联系开发者提出适用法律赋予的访问、更正、删除、限制处理或反对处理请求。对于由 Apple 或 Google 独立控制的数据，你可能需要直接使用其隐私工具或联系相应服务商。

7. 数据共享、出售与披露

我们不会出售你的本地收藏或播放历史。为提供分析、广告、购买处理与验证、客户支持、安全及法律合规，我们可能让上述服务提供方处理必要信息。法律要求、保护用户或开发者权利，或发生合并、收购及资产转移时，也可能依法披露或转移必要信息。

English

1. Scope and Provider

This policy applies to the iOS app “Qingqu Crosstalk / 青曲相声” (the “App”), provided by independent developer Shaolie Lin. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact l_charlie@163.com.

2. Data We Process

  • On-device data: favorites, recently played items, play counts, playback mode and speed, sleep-timer settings, and app preferences are primarily stored locally.
  • Analytics data: app launches, screen and feature interactions, playback and timer actions, advertising and purchase events, app-instance identifiers, device identifiers (which may include IDFV), and technical information about the app and device may be processed by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Advertising and connectivity data: Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process ad requests, impressions, clicks, device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, network information, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The App also sends lightweight connectivity checks to Google service endpoints to diagnose ad-network reachability; those requests include ordinary network information such as an IP address.
  • Purchase data: Apple processes product identifiers, transaction status, and receipts for the one-time remove-ads purchase and purchase restoration. The App’s receipt-validation request also sends your device’s identifierForVendor (IDFV) to Apple.
  • Support data: if you email us, we process your email address, message, device details, and any attachments you choose to provide so we can respond.

No account is required. The App does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data, and does not ask you to upload your own audio files. Do not send passwords, full payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information by email.

3. Local Data and Playback

The App’s audio files and track information are included with the App and read locally during playback. Favorites, recently played items, play counts, playback preferences, and related track information are stored in a local database. User-initiated audio may continue in the background, and system media controls may display the current track and control playback.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand app launches, feature interactions, playback, purchase, and advertising events. Google may automatically process app-instance identifiers, device identifiers such as IDFV, and device or app technical information.
  • Google Mobile Ads provides in-app advertising and may process device, advertising, network, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The App also sends lightweight connectivity checks to Google service endpoints to diagnose ad-network reachability; these requests include ordinary network information such as an IP address.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit processes product information, payment, purchase restoration, and transaction status. The App sends the app receipt and IDFV to Apple’s verification service to validate the remove-ads entitlement. We do not receive your full card details.

These providers process information under their own policies: Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Choices

Ad type and personalization may depend on applicable law, region, device and Google settings, and your choices. The App currently has no separate in-app advertising privacy-settings page. You can use Tracking settings under iOS Privacy & Security to manage cross-app tracking permission. Restricting tracking or personalized ads does not necessarily remove ads or stop every processing activity needed for ad delivery, security, and measurement. You may purchase Apple’s one-time remove-ads option.

6. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

  • Local track data and preferences generally remain until you reset App data or uninstall the App. Uninstalling usually deletes the local database; device backups may include App data depending on your Apple and iCloud settings.
  • Analytics, advertising, and technical logs are retained by the relevant providers according to operational, security, contractual, and legal requirements.
  • Apple retains purchase and transaction records under its policies. The App stores only the on-device status needed to provide the remove-ads entitlement.
  • You may email us to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection rights. For data independently controlled by Apple or Google, you may need to use their privacy tools or contact them directly.

7. Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your local favorites or listening history. Necessary information may be processed by the providers above for analytics, advertising, purchase handling and validation, support, security, and legal compliance. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

8. Children

The App is not designed as a child-directed service and does not ask users to submit real-world identity information within the App. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information to us by email may contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Security and International Processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the App’s scale and the nature of the data, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google, Apple, or email providers may process data outside your country under their policies, contracts, and applicable law.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change and will post the new effective date here. Privacy questions and rights requests: l_charlie@163.com.
