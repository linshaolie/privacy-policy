中文

1. 适用范围与运营者

本政策适用于 iOS 与 Apple Watch 应用“静念 / OneThought”（下称“本应用”）。本应用由独立开发者 Shaolie Lin 提供。如有隐私问题，请联系 l_charlie@163.com。

2. 我们处理的数据

数据用途处理位置
功德计数、历史、来源、称号与勋章、心愿内容与进度、收藏、播放状态、离线音频缓存和应用设置提供记录、心愿、播放、离线收听与个性化功能主要保存在设备本地；部分功德数据可在配对的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 间同步
随机生成的排行榜用户标识、排行榜昵称、今日/总功德及已释放功德提交和展示功德排行榜、识别当前排名发送到我们的排行榜服务并存储
应用启动、页面浏览、木鱼敲击里程碑、佛曲/白噪音/有声书播放、下载、收藏及广告奖励交互等事件；设备和应用技术信息统计使用情况、改进功能和衡量稳定性由 Google Analytics for Firebase 处理
国家或地区代码、语言、时区、系统版本、Firebase App ID、Bundle ID 及相关技术信息获取远程功能配置，例如是否启用开屏广告由 Firebase Remote Config 处理
广告请求相关的 IP 地址、设备标识符、广告数据、交互、大致位置、崩溃及性能信息（具体内容取决于 SDK 版本、设备设置、地区和同意状态）请求、展示、限制频率、衡量、分析和防止广告欺诈由 Google Mobile Ads 及其合作方处理
StoreKit 商品标识、价格与地区信息、购买结果及经 Apple 验证的交易信息展示供养项目、完成购买并发放对应功德由 Apple StoreKit 处理；本应用会读取完成交易所需的结果，但我们不接收银行卡或银行账户信息
IP 地址、请求时间、所请求的佛曲、白噪音、有声书、封面或榜单接口及常规网络日志提供在线目录、音频播放与下载、排行榜、安全防护和故障排查由我们的服务或相应内容源处理

我们不要求注册账号，也不会要求你提供真实姓名、电话号码或通讯录。请不要在排行榜昵称中填写敏感信息。

3. 设备权限与传感器

  • 运动传感器：在你开启摇动敲击时，本应用会在前台读取加速度数据以识别动作。原始运动数据不会被保存或上传。
  • 后台音频：用于你主动播放佛曲、白噪音或有声内容时继续播放。
  • Apple Watch 连接：用于在你的配对设备之间同步功德计数和操作状态。

4. 在线内容和第三方服务

  • Firebase Analytics：帮助我们了解功能使用情况。
  • Firebase Remote Config：获取轻量功能配置，例如是否启用开屏广告；该服务会处理国家或地区代码、语言、时区、系统版本和应用标识等技术信息。
  • Google Mobile Ads：展示开屏广告和激励广告。广告可能是个性化、非个性化、受限或仅技术投放，取决于适用法律、地区、同意状态和 Google 设置。
  • Apple StoreKit：加载供养商品、处理购买并验证交易。付款凭据和 Apple 购买记录由 Apple 管理。
  • 在线音频与图片源：当你浏览、播放或下载佛曲、白噪音、有声书和封面时，本应用或我们的代理服务会访问相应资源提供方。

第三方会按照其自身政策处理数据。你可以查阅 Google 隐私权政策、Firebase 隐私与安全说明和 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 广告与隐私选择

你可以通过 iOS 的隐私与跟踪设置限制广告标识符和跨 App 跟踪。在适用地区，如果应用显示 Google 的同意或隐私选项界面，你可以在其中管理广告相关选择。关闭个性化并不意味着不再显示广告。

6. 保留、删除和你的选择

  • 本地数据通常会保留到你在应用内删除内容、清除应用数据或卸载应用。佛曲、白噪音和有声书缓存可在“设置 → 离线缓存”中单独删除或全部清空。
  • 你可以编辑排行榜昵称；如需删除服务器上的排行榜记录，请发送邮件并提供应用中显示的排行榜昵称及可用于定位记录的必要信息。我们可能需要进一步核验请求。
  • 本应用不会另行持久化保存 StoreKit 交易明细；Apple 会依据其政策保留购买记录，并通过 Apple 账户与支持渠道提供管理。分析、广告和服务器日志的保留期限由我们或相应服务提供方依据安全、运营和法律需要决定。
  • 你可以关闭摇动敲击、停止使用在线内容，或通过系统设置管理相关隐私选项。

7. 数据共享与出售

我们不会出售你的心愿内容或本地功德历史。我们仅会为提供本应用、分析、广告、购买处理、安全和法律合规所需，与 Apple、Google 及上述内容或基础设施服务提供方处理或共享必要信息；在法律要求、保护权利或完成业务重组时，也可能依法披露必要信息。

8. 儿童、健康与宗教信息

本应用不是面向儿童定向设计的服务，也不会有意要求儿童提交真实身份信息。本应用提供的木鱼、冥想与心愿功能仅用于休闲和自我记录，不构成医疗、心理、宗教或其他专业建议。请勿在心愿或昵称中输入不必要的健康、宗教或其他敏感信息。

9. 安全与跨境处理

我们采用与应用规模相适应的技术和组织措施保护信息，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。第三方服务可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理数据，并依据其合同、政策和适用法律采取保护措施。

10. 政策更新

功能、服务提供方或法律要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并在本页公布新的生效日期。重大变化会在合理可行时通过应用内或其他适当方式提示。

11. 联系我们

English

1. Scope and Provider

This policy applies to the iOS and Apple Watch app “OneThought / 静念” (the “App”), provided by independent developer Shaolie Lin. Contact: l_charlie@163.com.

2. Data We Process

  • On-device data: merit counts and history, wishes and progress, titles and badges, favorites, playback state, offline audio files, and settings. This data is primarily stored locally; some merit data may sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Leaderboard data: a randomly generated user ID, display name, total and daily merit counts, and spent merit are sent to and stored by our leaderboard service.
  • Analytics and configuration: app launches, screens viewed, tap milestones, Buddhist music, white-noise and audiobook interactions, ad-reward interactions, and related app/device technical information may be processed by Google Analytics for Firebase. Firebase Remote Config also processes country/region code, language, time zone, OS version, Firebase App ID, bundle ID, and related technical information.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, interactions, approximate location, crash logs, performance, and related technical data depending on SDK version, device settings, region, and consent status.
  • Purchases: Apple StoreKit processes product IDs, price and storefront information, purchase results, and verified transaction information. The App reads the result needed to grant merit; we do not receive payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Network data: IP address, request time, requested Buddhist music, white noise, audiobook, artwork or leaderboard endpoint, and ordinary server logs may be processed by our service or content providers.

No account is required. We do not request your legal name, phone number, or contacts. Do not put sensitive information in your leaderboard name.

3. Sensors and Device Features

When shake-to-tap is enabled, accelerometer data is processed in the foreground to recognize gestures; raw motion data is not stored or uploaded. Background audio supports user-initiated Buddhist music, white-noise, and audiobook playback. Watch connectivity syncs relevant merit counts and actions between paired devices.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Mobile Ads, Apple StoreKit, and remote audio/image providers. These providers process data under their own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Ads and Choices

You can use iOS privacy and tracking settings to restrict advertising identifiers and cross-app tracking. Where applicable, use any Google consent or privacy-options interface presented by the App. Turning off personalization does not necessarily remove ads.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local data generally remains until you delete it, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Buddhist music, white-noise, and audiobook files can be removed individually or all at once under Settings → Offline Cache. To request deletion of a server-side leaderboard record, email us with the leaderboard name and the minimum information needed to locate the record; additional verification may be required. The App does not separately persist StoreKit transaction details; Apple retains purchase records under its policies and provides management through Apple account and support channels. Analytics, advertising, and server-log retention is governed by operational, security, legal, and provider requirements.

7. Sharing

We do not sell wish content or local merit history. Necessary information may be processed by Apple, Google, and the providers above for App functionality, analytics, advertising, purchase processing, security, or legal compliance, and may be disclosed when legally required or as part of a business reorganization.

8. Children and Sensitive Information

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly ask children for real-world identity information. Its meditation, wish, and wooden-fish features are for recreation and personal reflection, not medical, psychological, religious, or other professional advice. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

9. Security and International Processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the App’s scale, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party providers may process data outside your country under their policies, contracts, and applicable law.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when features, providers, or laws change and will post the new effective date here. Privacy and deletion requests: l_charlie@163.com.
