中文

1. 适用范围与运营者

本政策适用于 iOS 与 Apple Watch 应用“静念 / OneThought”（下称“本应用”）。本应用由独立开发者 Shaolie Lin 提供。如有隐私问题，请联系 l_charlie@163.com。

2. 我们处理的数据

数据用途处理位置
功德计数、历史、来源、称号、心愿内容与进度、收藏、播放状态和应用设置提供记录、心愿、播放与个性化功能主要保存在设备本地，并可在配对的 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 间同步
随机生成的排行榜用户标识、排行榜昵称、今日/总功德及已释放功德提交和展示功德排行榜、识别当前排名发送到我们的排行榜服务并存储
应用启动、页面浏览、木鱼敲击里程碑、音频播放/下载/收藏及广告奖励交互等事件；设备和应用技术信息统计使用情况、改进功能和衡量稳定性由 Google Analytics for Firebase 处理
广告请求相关的设备、广告、交互及大致位置等信息（具体内容取决于设备设置、地区和同意状态）请求、展示、限制频率、衡量和防止广告欺诈由 Google Mobile Ads 及其合作方处理
IP 地址、请求时间、所请求的在线音频或榜单接口及常规网络日志提供在线音频、排行榜、安全防护和故障排查由我们的服务或相应内容源处理

我们不要求注册账号，也不会要求你提供真实姓名、电话号码或通讯录。请不要在排行榜昵称中填写敏感信息。

3. 设备权限与传感器

  • 运动传感器：在你开启摇动敲击时，本应用会在前台读取加速度数据以识别动作。原始运动数据不会被保存或上传。
  • 后台音频：用于你主动播放经文、佛乐或有声内容时继续播放。
  • Apple Watch 连接：用于在你的配对设备之间同步功德计数和操作状态。

4. 在线内容和第三方服务

  • Firebase Analytics：帮助我们了解功能使用情况。
  • Firebase Remote Config：获取轻量功能配置，例如是否启用开屏广告。
  • Google Mobile Ads：展示开屏广告和激励广告。广告可能是个性化、非个性化、受限或仅技术投放，取决于适用法律、地区、同意状态和 Google 设置。
  • 在线音频与图片源：当你浏览或播放远程内容时，本应用或我们的代理服务会访问相应资源提供方。

第三方会按照其自身政策处理数据。你可以查阅 Google 隐私权政策和 Firebase 隐私与安全说明。

5. 广告与隐私选择

你可以通过 iOS 的隐私与跟踪设置限制广告标识符和跨 App 跟踪。在适用地区，如果应用显示 Google 的同意或隐私选项界面，你可以在其中管理广告相关选择。关闭个性化并不意味着不再显示广告。

6. 保留、删除和你的选择

  • 本地数据通常会保留到你在应用内删除内容、清除应用数据或卸载应用。
  • 你可以编辑排行榜昵称；如需删除服务器上的排行榜记录，请发送邮件并提供应用中显示的排行榜昵称及可用于定位记录的必要信息。我们可能需要进一步核验请求。
  • 分析、广告和服务器日志的保留期限由我们或相应服务提供方依据安全、运营和法律需要决定。
  • 你可以关闭摇动敲击、停止使用在线内容，或通过系统设置管理相关隐私选项。

7. 数据共享与出售

我们不会出售你的心愿内容或本地功德历史。我们仅会为提供本应用、分析、广告、安全和法律合规所需，与上述服务提供方处理或共享必要信息；在法律要求、保护权利或完成业务重组时，也可能依法披露必要信息。

8. 儿童、健康与宗教信息

本应用不是面向儿童定向设计的服务，也不会有意要求儿童提交真实身份信息。本应用提供的木鱼、冥想与心愿功能仅用于休闲和自我记录，不构成医疗、心理、宗教或其他专业建议。请勿在心愿或昵称中输入不必要的健康、宗教或其他敏感信息。

9. 安全与跨境处理

我们采用与应用规模相适应的技术和组织措施保护信息，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。第三方服务可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理数据，并依据其合同、政策和适用法律采取保护措施。

10. 政策更新

功能、服务提供方或法律要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并在本页公布新的生效日期。重大变化会在合理可行时通过应用内或其他适当方式提示。

11. 联系我们

隐私咨询、访问或删除请求：l_charlie@163.com

English

1. Scope and Provider

This policy applies to the iOS and Apple Watch app “OneThought / 静念” (the “App”), provided by independent developer Shaolie Lin. Contact: l_charlie@163.com.

2. Data We Process

  • On-device data: merit counts and history, wishes and progress, titles, favorites, playback state, and settings. This data is primarily stored locally and may sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Leaderboard data: a randomly generated user ID, display name, total and daily merit counts, and spent merit are sent to and stored by our leaderboard service.
  • Analytics: app launches, screens viewed, tap milestones, audio interactions, ad-reward interactions, and related app/device technical information may be processed by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process device, advertising, interaction, approximate location, and technical data depending on device settings, region, and consent status.
  • Network data: IP address, request time, requested audio or leaderboard endpoint, and ordinary server logs may be processed by our service or content providers.

No account is required. We do not request your legal name, phone number, or contacts. Do not put sensitive information in your leaderboard name.

3. Sensors and Device Features

When shake-to-tap is enabled, accelerometer data is processed in the foreground to recognize gestures; raw motion data is not stored or uploaded. Background audio supports user-initiated playback. Watch connectivity syncs relevant merit counts and actions between paired devices.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google Mobile Ads, and remote audio/image providers. These providers process data under their own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Ads and Choices

You can use iOS privacy and tracking settings to restrict advertising identifiers and cross-app tracking. Where applicable, use any Google consent or privacy-options interface presented by the App. Turning off personalization does not necessarily remove ads.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local data generally remains until you delete it, clear App data, or uninstall the App. To request deletion of a server-side leaderboard record, email us with the leaderboard name and the minimum information needed to locate the record; additional verification may be required. Analytics, advertising, and server-log retention is governed by operational, security, legal, and provider requirements.

7. Sharing

We do not sell wish content or local merit history. Necessary information may be processed by the providers above for App functionality, analytics, advertising, security, or legal compliance, and may be disclosed when legally required or as part of a business reorganization.

8. Children and Sensitive Information

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly ask children for real-world identity information. Its meditation, wish, and wooden-fish features are for recreation and personal reflection, not medical, psychological, religious, or other professional advice. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

9. Security and International Processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the App’s scale, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party providers may process data outside your country under their policies, contracts, and applicable law.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when features, providers, or laws change and will post the new effective date here. Privacy and deletion requests: l_charlie@163.com.
